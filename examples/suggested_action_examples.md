## Recurring Donor Segment

**Constituent Profile:**
- Name: David Park
- Monthly Gifts: $25/month for 8 consecutive months
- Total Given: $200
- Engagement: Low (no interactions outside of giving)

**Generated Action:**
```
Action: Mail David a handwritten thank-you card with a custom "Sustainer Impact Report"
showing his cumulative 8-month impact in infographic format, and include a personal photo
of one client/beneficiary with a brief story, arriving before his next monthly gift processes.

Timing: Mail by Monday to arrive within 5-7 business days, timed to reach him before his
next monthly gift date.

Why: Recurring donors are the most valuable segment but often receive the least personalized
attention. David's consistent $25/month with low engagement suggests he's committed but
potentially unaware of his cumulative impact ($200 = significant over time). Physical mail
stands out for monthly donors used to automated email receipts. Timing before his next gift
reinforces his decision to continue.

Key Points:
• Handwrite envelope and card - no printed labels
• Infographic shows "8 months = 40 meals served" or equivalent concrete impact
• Include specific beneficiary story: "Meet Maria - your support helps provide..."
• Add personal note: "David, your consistent monthly support is the foundation of our work"
• No ask, no upgrade request - pure stewardship
• Follow with "Sustainer-only" email update series (quarterly) to maintain engagement

- Age: 68
- Lifetime Giving: $12,500
- Giving Pattern: Annual gifts of $1,000-1,500 for past 10 years

```

## Response Format

All suggested actions follow this consistent structure:

1. **Action**: One clear, specific sentence describing what to do
2. **Timing**: Exact timeframe for implementation
3. **Why**: Data-driven rationale for this recommendation
4. **Key Points**: 3-7 tactical bullet points with specifics

This format ensures every recommendation is:
- ✅ Specific (not vague)
- ✅ Time-bound (clear deadline)
- ✅ Stewardship-oriented (relationship-focused)
- ✅ Donor-centric (benefits the donor)
- ✅ Non-generic (tailored to individual context)
